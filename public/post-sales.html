The Problem

Your buyers could be your biggest sales channel.
They are not. Yet.

Every developer without a structured referral and loyalty program is trapped in a high-cost, broker-dependent, transactional sales model.

High Cost of Sales

Broker commissions are eating your margins. Every single launch.

Broker and channel partner commissions represent 3 to 7 Cr per 100 Cr project in outbound sales cost. Every new launch requires the developer to activate the broker network again. Expensive, unpredictable, and not compounding. Meanwhile, referrals from existing buyers cost 95% less per booking.

"We spent 4.5% of revenue on broker commissions last year. That is 4.5 Cr on a single project. Referral bookings would have cost us a fraction." — VP Sales, Top 10 Indian Developer
No Loyalty Infrastructure

Your happiest buyers are sitting idle. Unengaged. Un-monetized.

Developers have no structured digital tool to reward, engage, and retain homebuyers post-booking. No loyalty program means no repeat purchase incentive for investors, no NPS-building engagement during construction, and no referral activation at possession, the highest-delight moment in the buyer journey.

"Our buyers love us. But we had zero systems to channel that love into referrals. We were leaving money on the table every single quarter." — Head of Loyalty, Leading Mumbai Developer
Home Loan Revenue Left on the Table

Banks pay commissions. You are earning nothing.

Developers informally refer buyers to banks and earn nothing structured in return. Banks pay 8,000 to 30,000+ per disbursed home loan to structured referral partners. A developer with 1,000 active buyers at 60% home loan uptake is leaving 50L to 1.8Cr per year in commission revenue uncaptured.

"We were casually referring buyers to banks for years. Post Sales structured this into a revenue line. In Year 1, our home loans team generated 1.1 Cr in commissions." — CFO, Runwal Group

Features

One platform.
Every revenue lever. Activated.

Post Sales turns every satisfied homebuyer into a revenue-generating brand advocate through referrals, loyalty, and home loan commissions.

Customer Mobile App
A white-labelled buyer app that puts the developer's brand in every homebuyer's pocket. Buyers access their unit details, payment schedule, construction updates, documents, referral hub, support, and home loan assistance — all in one branded experience. Deployed in weeks, not months.
Customer Journey Dashboard
Lifecycle engine from booking to registration to possession with guided next actions, progress tracking, and referral prompts at each completion milestone. Buyers who can see their journey don't panic. They trust, and they refer.
Referral & Loyalty Engine
Gamified referral hub with tier-based rewards (Silver, Gold, Platinum), wallet and points system, shareable referral cards for WhatsApp and Instagram, AI referral propensity engine, real-time reward tracking, and leaderboard. Increase referral booking rate from 2 to 5% baseline to 10 to 20%.
Construction Transparency
Real-time construction updates with verified images, videos, and milestone reports published directly to each buyer's app. Buyer anxiety drops 60% when they can see verified progress. RERA complaints reduce by 45%.
Support & Case Management
AI chatbot handles common queries instantly. RM direct calling for complex issues. Scheduled video calls for walkthroughs. CRM-integrated cases with CSAT tracking. Referral opportunities surfaced proactively through support interactions.
Developer Campaign Studio
Self-serve campaign management for the developer's loyalty team: buyer segmentation, referral bonus campaigns, tier-upgrade communications, A/B testing, and real-time performance dashboard. Run campaigns in minutes without depending on the vendor.
Home Loan Commission Module
In-app home loan enquiry, bank partner matching, application status tracking, commission dashboard for the developer's Home Loans team, and monthly revenue reconciliation. Generate 80L to 3Cr in annual home loan commission revenue.
