The Real Problem

Your Facility Feedback is Leaking Every Day.

Verbal complaints, WhatsApp messages, paper registers. They vanish before anyone acts. Issues repeat. Tenants churn. SLAs go unmeasured.

01 - Feedback Vanishes

Feedback is raised and forgotten in hours.

Verbal complaints, WhatsApp messages, paper forms. No channel routes feedback into a trackable system. By the time a manager hears about it, the tenant has already formed an opinion about your property.

72 hrs avg before issue escalation
02 - No Accountability

No SLA. No TAT. Nobody is accountable.

Without a system that converts every complaint into an assigned ticket with turnaround time and escalation rules, SLA breaches go undetected. FM vendor disputes have no audit trail.

Only 28 percent of complaints reach resolution
03 - Data on Vendor Cloud

Your tenant data lives on someone else's server.

Most SaaS helpdesk tools store occupant data on vendor infrastructure. Under India's DPDP Act and GDPR, that is legal exposure your enterprise clients and REIT investors will not accept.

DPDP Act compliance risk post-2025
