The Real Problem

Quality control is broken.
You already know it.

Construction teams still run snagging on paper, WhatsApp, and Excel. That is not a process. That is a liability waiting to surface.

01   Paper Kills Projects
Snag Lists on Paper Get Lost. WhatsApp Chains Get Buried.
Your site inspector documents a defect on a clipboard. Photographs it on a personal phone. Sends it to a WhatsApp group. Three weeks later, no one can confirm it was fixed. Rework costs run 5 to 15 percent of total project value when there is no structured audit trail from snag to closure. Without digital ownership, every defect is a repeat conversation.
Industry avg rework cost: 8% of project value
02   Your Data Is Not Yours
Every Competitor Stores Your Project Data on Their Cloud.
Procore, Autodesk ACC, Novade. All of them hold your inspection records, snag histories, and handover documents on servers you do not own or control. For large Indian developers and government-linked EPC contractors, this is a growing compliance and security exposure as data residency regulations tighten. No other snagging platform has solved this. Snag 360 has.
100% of competitors store your data on their servers
03   RERA Exposure
A Missed Snag at Handover Becomes a RERA Case. And a Brand Crisis.
Under India's RERA, developers carry five years of structural defect liability from the date of handover. A customer walkthrough that surfaces a defect your team missed is not just an embarrassment. It is a legal exposure. Without a stage gate system that blocks premature unit delivery, one oversight cascades into complaints, penalties, and brand damage.
5-year RERA defect liability starts at handover
