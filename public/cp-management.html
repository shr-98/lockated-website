The Solution

One platform. Every
CP touchpoint.

From the moment a broker discovers your scheme to the day they redeem their Gold-tier reward — CP Management owns the entire journey.

01

Self-Service DIY Onboarding

CPs register themselves — temporary or permanent — with document upload and real-time RERA verification. No developer team intervention. Walk-in CPs submit leads within minutes, not weeks. Onboarding TAT drops from 21 days to under 48 hours. Every week saved is a week your CP isn't sending inventory to a competitor.

02

Real-Time Financial Visibility

Direct SAP integration gives CPs live status on every invoice — submitted, verified, in processing, paid. No calls, no WhatsApp chasing, no month-end surprises. Cash flow certainty is a loyalty engine in itself. This is the primary reason top CPs choose to prioritise one developer over another.

03

Gamified Ladder Schemes

Rule-based Bronze, Silver, and Gold tier progression with animated milestone tracking. CPs see exactly where they stand and precisely what they need to close to unlock the next level. CPs on active gamified ladders close 18–25% more deals in the quarter the scheme goes live.

04

One-Click Collateral Distribution

Personalised, branded collateral shared at scale via WhatsApp and email — with full lead attribution back to the CP. For the first time, you will know which collateral drives leads and which shares convert. CP marketing reach grows from 30 contacts to 120+ per month.

05

SAP & Salesforce Native Integration

Production-grade, bidirectional integration with the two dominant enterprise systems in Indian real estate. Invoice push to SAP without manual data entry. SFDC sync ensures CP records, bookings, and cases stay accurate in real time. No middleware, no reconciliation sprints.

06

End-to-End CP Loyalty Engine

Wallet, redemption store, and tier management in one platform — vouchers, travel, lounge access, experiences, merchandise, and encashment. Developers with structured loyalty programs retain 30–40% more active CPs year-on-year. It transforms the CP relationship from transactional to structural.
