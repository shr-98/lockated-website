The Real Problem

Possession day is not the end.
But for most developers, it feels like it.

After the keys are handed over, the relationship collapses. No channel, no data, no leverage. And every day that passes is another referral lost.

Post-Possession Blackout

Your residents moved in. Your relationship moved out.

After possession, developers have zero structured touchpoint with residents. WhatsApp groups go silent. Complaints go to RWA committees. Referrals go untapped. The most expensive customer you ever acquired — nurtured through years of pre-sales — is now completely dark.

"We spent 18 months and 3 Cr building trust with our buyers. After possession, we had no way to stay in their lives. Every one of them was a dormant referral we couldn't activate." — VP CRM, Top Mumbai Developer
Operational Chaos

Your FM team runs a community on WhatsApp, Excel, and hope.

Visitor logs are paper registers. Complaints are WhatsApp messages. CAM bills are manually generated. Staff attendance is word-of-mouth. There is no SLA, no escalation, no audit trail — and your facility manager is drowning in coordination tasks that should be automated. One building. Seven tools. Zero integration.

"Our FM head was spending 4 hours a day just coordinating on WhatsApp. No dashboards, no SLAs, no compliance records. It was a liability waiting to happen." — COO, Leading Pune Developer
Referral Revenue Left on the Table

Satisfied residents are your highest-ROI lead source. You're not using them.

Channel partners charge 5 to 7% of project value per booking. A referral from a satisfied resident costs a fraction — but only if you have the infrastructure to capture, track, and reward it. Without a formal referral engine, every happy homeowner is a missed sales channel. And your CP commission bill compounds every launch.

"We had hundreds of residents who loved the project. No system to channel that love. We were paying brokers for leads we could have generated ourselves." — Sales Head, Bengaluru Developer

Features

Post Possession replaces every fragmented community tool with one white-labelled platform that connects residents, FM teams, and your developer brand.

White-Labelled Community App
Your brand. Your app. Your colours, your logo — on every resident's home screen. No Lockated branding. No third-party app diversion. All resident data stored exclusively on your own servers. Full data sovereignty, full brand ownership. Deployed on Android and iOS with rolling developer banners for new project launches.
Entire Customer Lifecycle
From pre-possession CRM to post-possession loyalty — one continuous relationship. Resident referrals tracked and rewarded automatically. New project launches pushed to existing communities as warm leads. Loyalty programs, wellness events, and offers that keep your brand alive in residents' daily lives — years after keys are handed over.
Integrated Community, FM & Security
Replace 7 disconnected tools with one platform. Community management, facility operations, CAM billing, visitor management, and gate security — all in a single login. Five-layer escalation matrix, PPM checklists with QR/NFC dual-control, automated CAM invoicing, real-time BI dashboards. One source of truth for every team.
25+ Visitor Management Features
Pre-authorised guest entry, OTP and IVR approval for unexpected visitors, child safety alerts, panic button, guard app offline mode for low-connectivity zones, e-Intercom HD video, overstay alerts, ANPR number plate recognition, and boom barrier API integration. Best-in-class gate security without expensive standalone security platforms.
Zero Unauthorised Vendors
Every service provider in your community is curated and approved by you. On-premise services marketplace — housekeeping, laundry, pest control, salons — all vetted, all revenue-tracked. No third-party apps competing for your residents' attention. No data leakage to competitor platforms. Your ecosystem, your revenue, your control.
Enterprise FM Operations
Asset tagging with QR codes, warranty alerts, PPM/AMC digital checklists, compliance tracker, permit-to-work, vendor evaluation, CAPA reporting, cost approval workflows, inventory management, operational audits — all in one FM console. Compliance-grade documentation for every maintenance task, audit-ready at any moment.
